# Beyond SEO: SALT Online Store SEO work brief

Date: 2026-10-23
Website: https://www.saltonlinestore.com/
Storefront: Shopify; audience United States; American English.

## Purpose
Audit and propose improvements to technical SEO, on-page SEO, Google discoverability and answer-engine discoverability. A GitHub document does **not** automatically grant access to Shopify, Google Search Console or theme deployments.

## Initial public-site observations
- The homepage search-facing title presents broad product categories (“Shop Cookware, Clothing, Decor & Gifts | Saltonlinestore”). Confirm the current title, meta description, H1 and rendered body text before changes.
- The public New Arrivals page at /collections/new-arrivals presents the collection title “New Arrivals Collection | Latest Trends - Saltonlinestore.” Check for current title/description duplication and confirm products match the collection intent. The retrieved page version may be older than today's store.
- The collection navigation is extensive. Check duplicate/category overlaps, crawling, and internal-link clarity without renaming collections.

## Required audit and deliverables
1. Crawl homepage, major collections and representative products; log URL, date, HTTP status, indexability, canonical, title, meta description, H1, structured data, image-alt issues and internal links.
2. Check Shopify robots.txt and sitemap.xml, Search Console indexing data (only if connected), redirects, 404s, faceted URLs and duplicate product variant paths.
3. Check mobile Core Web Vitals, layout shift, image size, JS errors and accessibility.
4. Evaluate real product collections for helpful US-English intent-driven copy, concise page titles, unique descriptions, schema and AEO-style FAQs where justified. No keyword stuffing or invented product claims, reviews or ratings.
5. Audit collection banners using Shopify collection ID and handle plus actual product images. Requested priority: New Arrivals, Women's Jewelry, Women's Sunglasses, Women's Maxi & Midi Dresses, Women's Bags and Watches, Women's Accessories, Weights, Wall Lights, Wall Art, Sunglasses and Stationery. Skip “Wall Under 49.” Preserve existing good simple banners for Gifts, Holiday and planners.
6. Produce an evidence-led action register: page/ID/handle, before snapshot, verified defect, recommendation, priority, effort, owner, approved final asset/copy and post-change QA.
7. Report baseline rankings, impressions, CTR and conversion only when verified through Search Console/Shopify; no guarantees of Google rank.

## Strict change control
- Audit and prepare drafts first. No automated live changes without review/authorization.
- Do NOT alter Shopify collection names, handles, product assignments, prices, inventory, menus, policies, or reviews.
- Do NOT make banner changes to excluded collections; do not replace already-correct visuals.
- Banner design: match collection merchandise and simple existing SALT theme, minimal text; descriptive alt text and page HTML should carry SEO meaning.
- Verify every banner by exact collection ID/handle; QA desktop and mobile before any publication.
- Never claim GitHub can deploy a Shopify theme unless the integration is verified.
